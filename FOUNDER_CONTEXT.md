# StudentOS Founder Context

## Mission

Help every student know their next best step.

## Vision

Become the most trusted AI companion for students.

## Problem

Millions of students are confused about careers, scholarships, internships, skills, and opportunities. Information is scattered and generic.

## Solution

StudentOS uses AI to understand each student personally and provide:

- Personalized career guidance
- Verified opportunities
- Weekly action plans
- Skill roadmaps
- Resume improvement
- Long-term progress tracking

## Core Principles

1. Student First
2. Trust over hype
3. Never invent opportunities
4. Explain recommendations clearly
5. Encourage action, not information overload

## Target Users

- Inter Students
- Degree Students
- B.Tech Students

## MVP

Input:
Student goal + education + interests

Output:
- Next Best Step
- Skills
- Opportunities
- Weekly Plan

## North Star

Every login helps one student make one better decision.

Building the AI Brain and first working prototype.
